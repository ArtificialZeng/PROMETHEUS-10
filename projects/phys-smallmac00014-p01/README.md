## Frozen scientific claim

For the dimensionless homogeneous-field magnetic Laplacian H_{a,b}=(-i nabla-A_b)^2 on every concentric annulus A_a={a<r<1}, with 0<a<1, b>0, no added harmonic Aharonov--Bohm flux, and respectively magnetic Neumann or Dirichlet conditions on both boundary circles, the globally ordered eigenvalues counted with multiplicity satisfy mu_3(A_a,b) <= lambda_1(A_a,b).
