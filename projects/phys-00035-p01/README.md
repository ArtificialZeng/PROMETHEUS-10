## Frozen scientific scope

For the original phase-free unnormalized unit-edge H_s, every integer k>=100 and every real theta in [1/2,2], eta=theta/k, s=sqrt(1-eta), have simple full ground and first excited energies in conserved-number N=2 and N=3 after X_a X_b. Every full N>=4 sector obeys lambda_N<lambda_3-(N-3)eta/2 for B=-X_a X_b H X_a X_b; complete internal N2 modes and N0/N1 are also excluded. Original all-graph/all-s conjecture remains unresolved.
