## A stationary normal conductor, with no auxiliary reservoirs

There are exactly three external reservoirs. Each lead is a semi-infinite normal nearest-neighbor chain with real hopping -10000 and onsite 20000 in epsilon=k_B T_ref units. Its one-channel propagating band is 0<E<40000. The boundary mode is outgoing/retarded. Solving the surface recurrence t^2 g^2-(E-2t)g+1=0 with negative imaginary part gives the surface Green function in the frozen model; independently, the outgoing Bloch relation E=2t-2t cos(k) gives g=-exp(ik)/t. These representations were rebuilt, not supplied as cached numerical data, in the new checker.

The sample is finite: three 128-site filter branches and three flux-ring sites. All diagonal terms are real; filter and contact hoppings are real and nonzero; the ring is i Gamma_c(P-P^T), which is Hermitian. Its nonzero Peierls loop phase breaks time reversal but neither particle-number conservation nor Hermiticity. The dot graph is time independent, normal, and noninteracting. Every join inside it is a Hamiltonian coupling, not a probe or controller. Possible bound sample modes outside the lead band make no extensive-in-time contribution to reservoir currents or their steady full counting statistics. A stationary occupation of these bound modes can be chosen without changing the scattering result.

The exact lead contact obeys Gamma(E)=-Im Sigma(E)>0 in the open band. The outer onsite compensation is E0-Re Sigma(E0). Thus the outer resolvent diagonal is delta+Re Sigma(E0)-Sigma(E), and its real contact change is exactly linear in delta, not an expansion. Dividing by 0.01 maps it to q-aq+i gamma(q) in the interval kernel. With q=(E-5000)/0.01, gamma_f=1/[2 sin(pi/256)], g_c=20000000, lambda^2=gamma_f g_c, and the contact coefficient is a=(0.01 gamma_f)/sqrt(5000*35000). Every coefficient agrees with the fixed physical model; no wide-band substitution or small-width truncation is used.

## Unitarity, channels, and well-defined integrals

Let P_o select the three outer sites and Gamma be the common positive broadening. The retarded finite resolvent satisfies

    G-G^dagger=-2i Gamma G P_o G^dagger.

For S=I-2i Gamma G_outer, this identity gives S S^dagger=I exactly. The retarded matrix is nonsingular in the open band: a zero mode would have zero norm on the absorbing outer sites by its imaginary quadratic form; the nonzero branch hoppings then force every filter amplitude, and finally every core amplitude, to vanish. Interval denominators that include zero therefore indicate insufficient interval resolution; they can safely use the physical transmission bounds [0,1].

There is one channel in each lead inside the band and zero propagating channels outside. This supplies the source's finite channel projectors. The single-reservoir extracted count at a fixed energy is X=n_in,0-n_out,0 in {-1,0,1}. Its probabilities are nonnegative because they are sums of squared Slater minors times independent reservoir occupations. Hence exp(-|s|)<=M_E(s)<=exp(|s|); all currents, heats, CGFs at finite real s, and their needed derivatives have finite band integrals. At band endpoints only measure-zero limiting values are required.

All temperatures equal T_ref>0. The same finite chemical potentials (5001,4997,4976)epsilon are used at every energy, so f_0>f_1>f_2 for finite E. At E=5000, the exact scattering is the forward cyclic permutation up to phases, with T_02=1. Both nonzero count probabilities there are strictly positive; continuity provides a positive-width physical interval. The counted mean is already positive and needs no orientation change. Its reversal j=-J lies strictly within the conjectured domain for every positive finite psi because coth[sigma/(2 psi k_B J)]>1. The global psi_0 bound and diagonal upper limit are established in the stable analytic arguments.

## Actual total entropy and signs

S_ab maps incident b into outgoing a, and the extracted count sign matches the original Levitov determinant. Source J_a=f_a-sum_b |S_ab|^2 f_b is exactly the event-averaged extracted count. Every source heat is Q_a=(E-mu_a)J_a/h under the energy integral. Particle and energy conservation give the exact isothermal identity sigma/k_B=sum_a (mu_a-E0)J_a in the declared dimensionless units; this includes all three reservoir heats.

The one-channel counting reduction is exact at every energy, independently of numerical point tests. Condition the incoming diagonal Fermi ensemble on n_in,0=1. Other incoming modes retain occupations f_b and zero coherences, so the expectation of the binary outgoing mode n_out,0 is |S_00|^2+sum_(b!=0)|S_0b|^2 f_b. The conditional probability n_out,0=0 is one minus this expectation. Row unitarity therefore gives p_+=f_0 sum_(b!=0)|S_0b|^2(1-f_b). Conditioning on n_in,0=0 similarly gives p_-=(1-f_0)sum_(b!=0)|S_0b|^2 f_b. These conditionings are the incoming occupation measurement underlying the source determinant and Slater-event expansion. Thus M=1+p_+(exp(s)-1)+p_-(exp(-s)-1) exactly; coherence between other channels introduces no missing term in this binary selected-mode marginal.

The off-diagonal scattering representation also follows by exact finite-dimensional algebra. Eliminating the filters gives eta=C/D for the core resolvent. For M_core=eta I-i g_c(P-P^T), det(M_core)=eta(eta^2-3g_c^2), and its (0,1) inverse cofactor is -g_c^2-i g_c eta=-i g_c(eta-i g_c). The outer off-diagonal Green function has filter factor lambda^2 H/D^2, with H the product of filter hopping-squared. Cancelling the D factors yields a numerator proportional to C-i g_c D and denominator C(C-sqrt(3)g_c D)(C+sqrt(3)g_c D). Its squared scattering modulus is exactly u in the interval kernel; the conjugate-sign cofactor gives v. The finite filter D is nonzero in the open band by the same absorbing-endpoint Jacobi argument. These are identities for the whole physical band, not fitted relations from the declared check grid.

The identical branches make the transmission matrix circulant. Write u=T_01, v=T_02, j_u=f_0-f_1>0, j_12=f_1-f_2>0, and j_v=j_u+j_12. The current vectors associated with u and v are (j_u,j_12,-j_v) and (j_v,-j_u,-j_12). Their actual entropy densities are respectively 25j_u+21j_12 and 4j_u+25j_12. They are positive; neither is a substituted forward–backward stochastic entropy. The independent checker recomputes event currents, Fock probabilities, the original determinant, and unitarity to numerical discrepancies below 1e-130 at its 23 declared points.
