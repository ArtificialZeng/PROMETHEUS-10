## Frozen scientific scope

For every strictly positive normalized binary probability table P with det(sqrt(P)) nonzero, consider the canonical two-copy purification in the fixed auxiliary split R=S=C^4 and U(t)=exp(i t H) on RS. On the 112-real-dimensional Hermitian-generator subspace with no first-order null-to-null Schmidt block, S(AR;t)=2 h2((1+sqrt(1-4 det(sqrt(P))^2))/2)+c_P(H)t^2+o(t^2). The exact quadratic form c_P is nonnegative with kernel precisely H=H_R tensor I+I tensor H_S, a 31-dimensional local auxiliary-unitary gauge. Thus its 81-dimensional quotient is strictly positive. The phase sector has the explicit sum-of-squares form and the controlled-rotation sectors the graph-Laplacian forms in quadratic-form.md. Outside this subspace, the positive leading t^2 log(1/t^2) coefficient implies entropy increase along every fixed non-gauge exponential-unitary path for sufficiently small nonzero t. This does not assert a common quantitative neighborhood, global additivity, boundary/product-table coverage, or a result for larger auxiliary splits.
