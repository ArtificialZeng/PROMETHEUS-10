## Frozen scientific claim

For the no-dagger three-output Bell functional I3 of arXiv:2606.21362v3 Eq. (6), every tensor-product strategy of arbitrary local dimension with three-output projective measurements satisfies I3<=4. The bound is attained by the stated maximally entangled qutrit strategy. Equivalently, 4*1-I3 has the submitted exact rank-16 Q(omega) noncommutative SOS on the 35-word 1+AB span. Equality rigidity, self-testing, robustness and randomness certification are not claimed.
