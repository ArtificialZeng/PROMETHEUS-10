# Redistribution scope check

Checked 2026-09-13 UTC for the files selected in `publication.json`.

- The manuscript TeX, bibliography database, handoff, reproduction
  instructions, requirements file, Python programs, and generated diagnostic
  logs are project-authored materials.
- The manuscript contains no copied figure, table, or extended passage from a
  third party. It uses ordinary scholarly citations and short bibliographic
  metadata.
- No downloaded article PDF, cached article text, browser record, audit tree,
  job log, credential, or private account artifact is selected for the handoff
  archive.
- The author must choose a license for public code/data distribution and
  confirm publisher permissions at actual submission. This check only limits
  the local bundle to materials that can be reviewed for redistribution; it is
  not legal advice.
