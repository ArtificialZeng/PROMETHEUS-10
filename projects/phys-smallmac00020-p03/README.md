## Frozen scientific claim

For the finite open extended-SSH family H_N with u2=1, u0=r^2, u1=2r and r in \[1/5,4/5\], the exact confluent four-state pencil has positive energies r^{N+1}/phi_{1,N} and r^{N+1}/phi_{2,N} with the finite sums defined in the evidence. For every integer N>=61 the exact two positive in-gap energies obey the explicit uniform error functions R_a(N,r), these errors are relative o(1) uniformly in r, the leading laws are E_{N,1}=(1-r^2)r^{N+1}/N\[1+o(1)\] and E_{N,2}=N(1-r^2)^3r^{N-1}\[1+o(1)\], and no third positive level lies below the bulk-band distance (1-r)^2.
