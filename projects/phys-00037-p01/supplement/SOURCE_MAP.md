# Primary-source map for the local draft

The paper cites the specific author versions below. Version histories were
checked on 14 October 2026. A bounded exact-claim search did not identify an
equivalent three-mode normal-Hessian theorem; this is not an exhaustive
priority claim or proof of present openness of the original global conjecture.
Full third-party papers are not included in this portable supplement.

Bravyi's archived PDF has an internal date of 16 October 2018, while its arXiv
history lists only v1 from 2005. Bibliographic dating follows that version
history; the file date does not establish a later arXiv revision or journal
publication. The four other date/version pairs likewise identify the checked
author versions. Some have published journal counterparts, but the checked
body and source loci here remain the archived versions.

The new computer-assisted argument is specified in the manuscript and the
complete original rational witness. Source reproduction establishes the
legal complete channel, rather than substituting covariance-only data.
No novelty is assigned to the standard Gaussian definition, logarithmic
derivative, polynomial basis, or known Gaussian entropy formula.
