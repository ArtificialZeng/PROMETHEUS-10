## Frozen scientific claim

Their unique nonzero continuation exists for every k>=3 at J_k=+/-3^(-(k-3)/2), x_k=|J_k| and lambda_k=|J_k|/2; the generic flat multiplicity c_k=(3^(k-2)-1)/2 becomes c_k+1 and |J_(k+1)/J_k|=1/sqrt(3). On fixed I_+=\[1/4,1/2\] and I_-=-I_+, Delta_k is zero iff k=5; for k=3,4 and k>=6 it obeys the explicit positive coefficient-norm bound in evidence/fixed-interval-gap-salvage-result.md. Separately named moving windows W_k^+=\[J_k/2,3J_k/2\] and W_k^-=-W_k^+ have zero minima for every k>=3. The characteristic polynomial is generated by the signed three-scalar recurrence and exact quotient in the evidence.
