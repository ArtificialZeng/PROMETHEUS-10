# phys-00007-p06

**The sharp overlap threshold for antidistinguishability of five pure states**

Result class: `proofs`; original question: `proved`; release: `draft_ready_author_review_required`.

## Background

The overlap-only universal guarantee for ideal single-copy five-state zero-error exclusion is sharp. Arbitrarily small increases above sqrt(3/8) admit non-excludable states, with certified positive uniform-prior error lower bound s*eta/5.

## Frozen scientific scope

For five arbitrary normalized complex pure states in C^5, allowing dependence, the infimum t5 of maximum pairwise overlap modulus over non-antidistinguishable tuples equals sqrt(3/8), with single-copy unrestricted PSD POVMs and zero effects permitted. An explicit rank-three family has every squared overlap m and is non-antidistinguishable for every real 3/8<m<2/5. Its exact physical dual certificate gives uniform-prior optimum exclusion error at least s*eta/5>0 as defined in evidence/five-state-proof.md. The published bound includes the boundary; this is an infimum approached from above, not an attained non-excludable minimum. No n>=6 or exact optimum error claim.

## Proposed contribution and limitations

An explicit rank-three five-state pure-state family with every squared pairwise overlap m in (3/8,2/5), and an exact positive-trace physical exclusion dual witness for every such m, proves t5=sqrt(3/8).
