# phys-00002-p12

**Sharp lower bounds on current cumulant ratios in Markov rings with an irreversible bond**

Result class: `partial-results`; original question: `unresolved`; release: `draft_ready_author_review_required`.

## Background

Validates the proposed third/fourth current-ratio transport diagnostics throughout an irreversible-bond classical single-cycle class, including strong backward excursions elsewhere. It identifies a rigorously safe boundary sector; all-positive finite-affinity universality remains unresolved.

## Original question

双向单环随机网络的负偏度、负峰度能否突破猜想界？

## Frozen scientific scope

For every finite simple nearest-neighbor time-homogeneous Markov ring with N>=3, finite forward rates u_i>0, nonnegative finite reverse rates w_i and at least one w_i=0, the long-time signed one-cycle count cumulant rates satisfy c3/c1>=-1/16 and c4/c2>=-(1+sqrt(5))/10. Here c1,c2>0; arbitrary initial law and any counted bond yield the same ratios. Both constants are sharp over unbounded finite N. The original strictly positive bidirectional conjecture remains unresolved; no finite-N extremizer or interior bound is asserted.

## Proposed contribution and limitations

An ansatz-free analytic proof of both winding-ratio lower bounds for every positive exponential-stage vector, mapped by the known passage-time theorem to every finite Markov ring with at least one irreversible bond and arbitrary remaining backward rates. Both constants are asymptotically sharp.
