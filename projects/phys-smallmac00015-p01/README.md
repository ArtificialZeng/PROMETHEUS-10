## Frozen scientific claim

In the frozen symmetric-splitting complete-graph model, for every integer N>=3, k0>0, finite offset vector, period T>0, and finite real Fourier drive, the weak coefficients satisfy A2>=2k0 B2. At finite offsets the inequality is strict whenever at least one energy difference varies in time; equality is exactly the zero-response class A2=B2=0. The coefficient 2 is optimal but is attained by a response-active system only at the excluded offset boundary, approached by x1=x2=1 and x3=...=xN=delta down to zero.
