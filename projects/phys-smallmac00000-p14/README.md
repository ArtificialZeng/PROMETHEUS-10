## Frozen scientific claim

At t=1, g=(1-i)/5, eta=1/5, mu=0 on the original odd-left-dimerized half-line, Q0=sqrt(1-rho^2) sum_(n>=0) rho^n alpha_(2n+1), rho=3(1-i)/10, has a unique exponentially localized parity-odd normalized first-order continuation Q1. No exponentially localized Q2 solves \[H0,Q2\]=-\[V,Q1\]. Thus there is no twice-differentiable exact commutator-zero-mode continuation from Q0 whose first two Taylor coefficients are exponentially localized and whose square normalization holds to first order.
