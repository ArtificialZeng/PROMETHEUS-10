# phys-00007-p09

**Local robustness of commuting-reflection encoding near the three-qubit W state**

Result class: `partial-results`; original question: `unresolved`; release: `draft_ready_author_review_required`.

## Background

Eight perfectly distinguishable messages from coordinated commuting product reflections remain possible under arbitrary sufficiently small pure-state perturbations of W3, with joint decoding.

## Frozen scientific scope

There exist epsilon>0 and C<infinity such that every normalized complex three-qubit pure-state ray with phase-fixed chart psi=(W3+eta)/sqrt(1+||eta||^2), <W3|eta>=0, ||eta||<epsilon admits a commuting-reflection encoding with the triangle graph and a local SU2 change V(theta)=exp(-i theta.sigma/2)q^tensor3 satisfying ||theta||<=C||eta||^(1/3). On psi(t)=(W3+t delta)/sqrt(1+13t^2), delta=V0^dag O V0 W3, an exact branch has theta=s v1+O(s^2), t=(40ab/39)s^3+O(s^4), whereas no C1 branch through V0 exists. No universal claim away from the neighborhood or numerical radius is asserted.

## Proposed contribution and limitations

An exact open neighborhood of arbitrary complex pure-state perturbations of W3 is encodable with the fixed triangle graph. A nonzero cubic reduced moment yields an anchored O(distance^(1/3)) encoder-control bound and cube-root continuation on a specified legal state path.
